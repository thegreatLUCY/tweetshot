🖼️ TweetShot

Blur faces & plates, add captions and arrows, crop and tweak light — before you post on X.

  1. Compose a tweet on X.com and attach one or more photos
  2. Click ✎ Edit on a preview (or Edit photo in the toolbar)
  3. Edit → Use in tweet. Batches step through image by image.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ S save · O compare
